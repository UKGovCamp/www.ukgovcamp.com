<h2 class="page-title" id="about-us">About us</h2>

100 of us first met in 2008 at Google's Victoria office. We discussed everything from e-voting, to vitual worlds, and the politics of the Freedom of Information (FOI).

We've met
[every year since](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1S6nemSPxSLrURGigaQZFKViWBoAhalpE2f0RtZ92Fpk/edit?gid=13#gid=13)
and now average 250 people per event, though are always over subscribed.

## UK Gov Camp is

- free to attend,
- led by attendees,
- run by volunteers.

## Attendees lead by:

- suggesting discussion topics,
- merging similar topics together,
- adding topics to the [session grid](https://j.mp/ukgovcamp).

## Then we discuss:

- each topic on the session grid,
- how it makes people feel,
- what we'll do next.
