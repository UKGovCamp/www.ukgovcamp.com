You voted, we listened. Following last year's community poll, we are bringing UKGovCamp 2027 to Manchester!

Here is what we are currently working on and how you can get involved.

### Join the Organising Team and Venue Search

To make the next event happen, we are recruiting volunteers and searching for a venue.

#### Organising Team Recruitment

We are looking for people to join us across two teams:

* Remote Team: Handling behind-the-scenes work, including logistics, ticketing, and general administration from anywhere.
* Local Team: Based in or around Manchester to lead on-site planning, conduct site visits, and manage local coordination.

Please send an email to [organisers@ukgovcamp.com](mailto:organisers@ukgovcamp.com) if you are interested in participating.

#### Venue Recommendations

We are actively hunting for a venue in Manchester that can accommodate **200 to 250 people**, with multiple breakout spaces for concurrent pitch sessions.

We would love suggestions for **Government buildings, civic spaces, or University campuses** close to public transport links. If you know of a space that fits the bill or have local contacts we should reach out to, please send your recommendations our way! Our email is [organisers@ukgovcamp.com](mailto:organisers@ukgovcamp.com).

### Digital Infrastructure Review

We are slowly reviewing our entire digital setup, including the UKGovCamp website, hosting, and wider tool ecosystem. The goal is to build a modern, accessible, and reliable digital home that better serves the community long-term.

### Legal Foundations Update

We are almost ready to officially register with Companies House as a CLG (Community Interest Company Limited by Guarantee). The Articles of Association are reviewed and approved by the trustees. This step gives us a solid legal structure to manage funding, grant distribution, and venue bookings as a dedicated non-profit.

### Membership Scheme Coming Soon

We will be announcing details of our new membership scheme very soon. Stay tuned for more updates on how to get involved!
