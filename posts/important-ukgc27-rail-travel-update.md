Some train journeys to and from Manchester will be affected by engineering works on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

### West Coast Main Line

All lines between Rugby and Stafford via Nuneaton will be closed on Saturday and Sunday from 09:30.   
  
Working services from London on Saturday include:
- Euston 05:58 > 09:05 Manchester Piccadilly 
- Euston 06:57 > 10:05 Manchester Piccadilly

Other services between London and Manchester may:
- divert,  
- require you change trains,
- take a bus replacement service.

### Transpennine Route Upgrade

Huddersfield station will be closed on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

TransPennine Express & Northern trains may be:  
- diverted (many via Brighouse and the Calder Valley),
- split into separate sections,   
- replaced by buses.

### North Wales Coast Line

All lines between Chester and Holyhead will be closed on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

Rail replacement buses operating west of Chester.
