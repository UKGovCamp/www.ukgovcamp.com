Thank you everyone who’s already entered our
[ticket lottery](https://luma.com/kwpj3osz). We’ll share the results on 1
December 2026.

We need your feedback about the following railway travel disruptions (if you
won’t use the railways, you can ignore this). You'll be asked the questions via
our ticketing system Luma.

## Saturday 16 January 2027 (the day of UKGovCamp)

### Travelling from London & the South (West Coast Main Line)

All lines between Rugby and Stafford via Nuneaton will be closed.

The only early morning, direct Avanti West Coast trains from London Euston to
Manchester Piccadilly are the:

- 05:58 arriving 09:05,
- 06:57 arriving 10:05 (we can start late to accommodate).

Other services between London and Manchester may divert, require you change
trains, or take a bus replacement service.

### Yorkshire & the North East (Transpennine Route Upgrade)

Huddersfield station will be closed, disrupting services between Stalybridge and
the Huddersfield/Dewsbury area.

- TransPennine Express & Northern: Trains will be diverted (many via Brighouse
  and the Calder Valley), split into separate sections, or replaced by buses
  between Stalybridge, Huddersfield, and Brighouse.
- Sheffield Services: Direct Hope Valley Line trains between Sheffield and
  Manchester Piccadilly are not affected.

### North Wales (North Wales Coast Line)

All lines between Chester and Holyhead (including the Conwy Valley line) will be
closed.

- Transport for Wales & Avanti West Coast: Trains between Manchester and Chester
  will run normally, with rail replacement buses operating west of Chester.

## Friday 15 January - the day before

- The Transpennine Route and North Wales Coast closures are already active all
  day on Friday.
- The West Coast Main Line closure between Rugby and Stafford does not begin
  until Saturday, 16 January.

## Sunday 17 January – the day after

- All Saturday works continue, plus all lines between Manchester Piccadilly and
  Wilmslow / Stoke-on-Trent via Stockport will be closed.
- Avanti West Coast, CrossCountry, Northern, and Transport for Wales services
  heading south will run to amended timetables, divert via Styal, or be replaced
  by buses.
