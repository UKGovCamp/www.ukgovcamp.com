October has flown by. Here is what the UKGovCamp organising team has been up to
this month.

### **Venue, date and tickets: nearly there**

UKGovCamp 2027 will be in Manchester in mid-January. We expect to confirm the
venue very soon, and we will announce the exact date at the same time.

Tickets will be available very shortly after that. Keep an eye on
[our Bluesky feed](https://bsky.app/profile/ukgovcamp.com) so you don't miss
them.

### **Becoming a non-profit company**

We are planning to become a non-profit company very soon. There is just one
thing standing in the way: we need permission from the Cabinet Office to use the
word "Gov" in a business name.

If you know someone very senior in the Cabinet Office who could help us move
this along, we would love to hear from you. Please
[email the organisers](mailto:organisers@ukgovcamp.com).

### **Tidying up our finances**

Behind the scenes, we are tidying up our finances. As part of that, we will
shortly be talking to HMRC to check whether we owe any back taxes, so that the
new organisation can start on a clean footing.

### **Joining up with GovCamps in Wales and Scotland**

We are connecting with the people who run
[GovCamp Cymru](https://www.govcamp.cymru/) and
[GovCamp Scotland](https://www.govcamp.scot/), so we can share what we learn and
support each other's events.
