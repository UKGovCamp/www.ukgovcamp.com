The wait is officially over. On Saturday, 16 January 2027, we will be hosting the 19th year of UKGovCamp at the Manchester Central Convention Complex (GMEX)!

Whether you are a regular who joins us every year or this is your very first time hearing about us, we would love to have you with us in Manchester.

### Never been to an unconference before?

If you are new to the unconference format, here is how it works:

There is no fixed schedule or speaker lineup set in advance. Instead, the sessions are pitched, chosen, and run by attendees on the morning of the event. This grassroots approach allows us to tackle the urgent, messy, and genuinely important topics on the day, with the shared goal of designing better outcomes for everyone who relies on public services.

The real magic comes from the serendipity of bringing people together from right across the public sector, civic tech, design communities, and beyond, to talk as equals, regardless of job title, seniority, or department. Everyone’s lived experience and perspective matters. It is this open, informal, and relaxed atmosphere that makes UKGovCamp so special.

And yes, in case you were wondering: food and drinks are fully provided throughout the day, thanks to the generous support of our sponsors. We also offer bursaries and special arrangements upon request. Funds for bursaries are limited, so we cannot guarantee one for every applicant, but we will always do our very best to help where we can.

### How the ticket lottery works

Because demand is always high and spaces fill up fast, we run a fair ticket lottery rather than a first-come, first-served rush.

Sign up to register your interest, and tickets will be allocated through a completely random draw.
